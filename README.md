# 📊 Call Center MIS Dashboard

## 🔹 Short Description / Purpose
This project provides a **Management Information System (MIS) dashboard** for analyzing call center performance. It helps track representative efficiency, customer satisfaction, call volumes, and revenue contribution. The purpose is to enable **data-driven decision-making** for workforce planning, performance evaluation, and customer experience improvement.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Excel / Pivot Tables** – for data aggregation and reporting  
- **Power BI (optional)** – for interactive dashboards and visualization  
- **Data Analysis Techniques** – pivoting, ranking, and performance metrics  

## 📂 Data Source
- **Internal Call Center Logs** containing:  
  - Call number, duration, representative ID  
  - Customer demographics (gender, age, city)  
  - Purchase amount & satisfaction rating  
  - Date, day of week, and duration bucket  

*(Example: “Call_7271 – Customer C0003, 116 mins, Rep R05, Purchase ₹128, Rating 4.9”)*

## ✨ Features / Highlights
- **Representative Performance Tracking**  
  - Call count, total amount, average rating, and 5★ calls per rep  
  - Ranking by call volume and revenue contribution  

- **Customer Insights**  
  - Segmentation by city (Cincinnati, Cleveland, Columbus)  
  - Gender and age distribution of callers  

- **Call Analytics**  
  - Duration buckets (Under 10 mins, 30–60 mins, 1–2 hours, >2 hours)  
  - Day-of-week trends for call volumes  

- **Revenue & Satisfaction Metrics**  
  - Total purchase amount across calls  
  - Average satisfaction rating (≈3.9 overall)  
  - Identification of high-value customers and repeat interactions  

- **Monthly Trends**  
  - Call distribution across Jan–Dec  
  - Seasonal variations in call activity
